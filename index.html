AI-Optimized Day-Ahead EV Charging Dispatch

Python · MILP · PuLP/CBC · Ontario TOU · V2G · BESS · Solar PV

Building a mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) model that schedules a DC fast-charging station one day ahead — jointly optimizing EV load curves, vehicle-to-grid (V2G) discharge windows, battery energy storage (BESS) cycling, and a 15 kW solar canopy against Ontario's three-tier time-of-use pricing. The model produces cost-optimal, grid-friendly dispatch decisions before any vehicle arrives.
